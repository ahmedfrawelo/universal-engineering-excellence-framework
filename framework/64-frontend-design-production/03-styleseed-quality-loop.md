# Styleseed Quality Loop

For a material visual review:

1. lock the intended visual grammar in `STYLESEED.md` and reference the authoritative `DESIGN.md` hash;
2. resolve the effective rules before implementation;
3. build inside the repository owner;
4. score the rendered result with `styleseed-design-review`;
5. revise concrete findings until the score is at least 80;
6. render and verify the final responsive, theme, RTL, accessibility, and interaction states.

The score is evidence, not completion. A result below 80 fails the design-production gate. A score of 80 or more still fails when the requested behavior or another selected UEEF gate is unproved.
